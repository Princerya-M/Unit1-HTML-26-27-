Egg omlette recipe

I choose this meal because its one of my favorite and quick to make meals.

  • Ingredients
  • Steps
  • Notes

Ingredients

  • 6 large eggs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Whisk: Beat eggs, milk, salt, and pepper in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat.
  3. Pour in eggs; push cooked edges to the center so liquid flows underneath.
  4. Sprinkle cheese over one half when eggs are mostly set.
  5. Fold the omelette in half and slide it onto a plate.

Notes

  1. Equipment Needed
    • Pan: Use an 8-inch non-stick skillet so the eggs slide out easily.
    • Spatula: Use a silicone spatula to fold the eggs without tearing them.
    • Bowl & Whisk: Use them to beat air into the eggs for a fluffy texture.
  2. Serving Suggestions
    • Carbs: Pair with buttered toast or crispy hash browns.
    • Greens: Balance the meal with sliced avocado or a simple side salad.
    • Meat: Serve alongside crispy bacon or breakfast sausage.
