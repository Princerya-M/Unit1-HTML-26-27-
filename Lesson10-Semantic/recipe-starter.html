Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients·Steps

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 egg
  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix the butter, sugar, and egg.
  3. Add the flour and chocolate chips.
  4. Place spoonfuls of dough on a baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 10-12 minutes.

Why This Recipe

I chose this recipe because chocolate chip cookies are easy to make and taste great. They are also a fun dessert to share with family and friends.

Fun fact: Chocolate chip cookies were invented in the 1930s.
